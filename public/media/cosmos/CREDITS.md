# Cosmic imagery and film

All stills are telescope observations, rendered in the source teams' chosen colors. Images are locally compressed to WebP and cropped for layout. The V838 image uses the September 2006 panel of the published comparison, cropped to omit its border and date. No endorsement is implied.
