## Hero generation prompt

Create a cinematic ultrawide 16:9 photorealistic CGI concept visualization for a frontier-industrial research website. A monumental, physically plausible orbital research station over Earth's curved blue atmospheric limb. Station occupies the RIGHT half: modular dark titanium cylinder with truss structures, precisely engineered radial circular ring and gold thermal foil, broad dark solar arrays receding in perspective. A tiny distant sun edge light, dramatic near-black space, Earth muted blue and clouds at bottom. LEFT half mostly clean near-black negative space for large white website headline overlay, no text in image. Aerospace engineering realism, exquisite hard-surface industrial material detail, 70mm IMAX feel, cool silver and charcoal palette, subtle electric blue atmosphere. Not fantasy, no neon, no logos, no words, no watermarks. Premium editorial art direction. This is clearly a speculative concept render, not a depiction of an existing installation.

The render is an illustration of a concept. Visual plausibility is not a validated engineering design.
