## Customer Objection Handling

### "Why not hire in-house?"

Because hiring a full product team takes time, costs more upfront, and slows early execution. Tera gives you immediate access to design, product, engineering, and AI capability in one team.

### "Why not use a cheap agency?"

Cheap agencies often optimize for deliverables, not outcomes. Tera is positioned around launch-ready execution, quality, transparency, and business impact.

### "We are still early."

That is exactly when speed matters most. Tera has entry-point offers that let early-stage teams validate quickly before overcommitting.

### "We only need one thing."

That is fine. Customers can start with strategy, a website, an AI integration, or an MVP and expand later if needed.
